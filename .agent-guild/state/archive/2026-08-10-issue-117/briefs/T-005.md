## Spec excerpt

Rewrite step 3 of `guild-core/workflows/retrospective/SKILL.md`, which today reads:

> ## 3. Offer to archive
>
> The next job reuses `.agent-guild/state/`. Offer to move this run's state to `.agent-guild/state/archive/<date>/` (get the date from the environment) so the record survives without colliding with the next constitution and task set.

"This run's state" never says what that includes, and `log/` reads as machine plumbing rather than as part of the record, which makes it the easy thing to leave behind. That is not hypothetical: one run's archive has no `log/` directory at all, so its seven vendor-call rows stayed in the live ledger and the next job appended on top of them. Two jobs' rows ended up in one file keyed by the same task ids, which is the defect this whole job exists to fix.

Rewrite the step so it enumerates what moves — `tasks/`, `verdicts/`, `disputes/`, `notes/`, `log/`, `briefs/`, plus `spec.md`, `constitution.md`, and `retrospective.md` — and says why `log/` belongs to the run's record rather than sitting outside it: the vendor-call ledger is the evidence #34 rules on, and a ledger left behind gets appended to by the next job.

This is prose a person follows while tired at the end of a job. Write it that way: an enumeration they can check themselves against, and one sentence of reason, not a warning label. Keep the step's existing voice; it is part of a skill body someone reads top to bottom.

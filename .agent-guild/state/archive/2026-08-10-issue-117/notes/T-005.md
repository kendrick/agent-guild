## Taste calls

**Three bullets, with `log/` on its own line.** The failing example in C-6 is a parenthetical a reader skims past, so burying `log/` mid-list next to `notes/` would have reproduced the defect in a slightly nicer font. Giving it its own bullet, glossed with what's actually inside it (`vendor-calls.jsonl` named outright), makes it a line a tired reader has to look at. The grouping mirrors the state map in `.agent-guild/CLAUDE.md`: message bus, log, top-level files.

**"Check `log/` last" rather than a warning.** The spec asked for an enumeration someone can check themselves against plus one reason, not a caution box. An imperative that tells the reader where to look last does the instructing; the sentence after it supplies the why.

**Named the consequence, not the issue number.** The reason lands as "a ledger left live is one the next job appends to—two jobs' rows in one file, keyed by the same task ids." That's the #17 incident stated as a mechanism, so the step still means something in a copied-in kit where nobody has read #34. Other guild-core prose does cite issue numbers as war stories, so I could have; the mechanism carries further.

**Trusted the spec's enumeration over the live directory.** `.agent-guild/state/` also holds `exhausted/`, and `PAUSED` and `STALLED.md` can exist. Those are user-cleared escape hatches, not the run's record, and the spec's list omits them. I left them out rather than inventing a rule about what stays behind. Flagging the ambiguity here rather than resolving it in the prose.

**Verified the ledger claim before writing it.** "Accounts for what every crossing cost and how the second opinions landed" is checked against `vendor-call.schema.json` (duration, tokens, cost, quota events, verified verdict artifacts) and against a real row in `log/vendor-calls.jsonl`, which is keyed by `task_id` — hence "the same task ids" being literally true across jobs.

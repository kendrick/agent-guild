### The lane's pinned identity does not match what the vendor reports

This is the second crossing to hit it. T-001 r0 saw the same `gpt-5.6` against the same pinned `gpt-5.6-terra` and *persisted* it rather than blocking, which is how the wrong value reached a verdict of record in the #34 corpus. This one blocked, as the contract requires.

So the two crossings disagree about what to do, and both cannot be right. Read together they say the defect is upstream of either: the vendor consistently reports a model string the lane does not expect. Either `guild-core/roles/checker-courier.md:58` pins a name the vendor never returns, or the vendor normalizes `gpt-5.6-terra` to `gpt-5.6` on the way back. Nothing in this job can tell which, because the courier is the only thing that sees the raw response and it does not keep it.

The consequence is not small. A courier that verifies identity strictly — which is what the role demands, for the good reason that #34's corpus turns on knowing which model produced an opinion — will `blocked` every crossing on this lane. A courier that does not verify strictly will book opinions under a model name that was never confirmed. The dual-check regime this job just made mandatory therefore has a lane that cannot currently produce a verified crossing, and the gate will accept the resulting `blocked` verdicts as discharge without ever surfacing why.

That is the sharpest thing this job learned about the multi-provider arc, and it is worth more than the crossing it cost.

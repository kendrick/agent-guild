Relaxed `_governs_plural_noun` to walk forward over up to `_MAX_GOVERNING_WORDS` (4) words after the count, asking only "does this word end in `s`" at each step — it never classifies or looks up the intervening words, so there's no adjective list and no suffix gate for C-1's run-time-invented modifiers to defeat. ## 2. R21 checker routing (C-3, C-4)

Added `"R21": PROOF` to `RULE_CLASS` and inserted `rule_R21` into `run_rules`'s `steps` tuple immediately after `rule_R7`, ahead of every heuristic (R2/R9/R10/R12').

Also updated the module docstring's rule-ordering table and the CON-audit skip list to mention R21, since the spec pointed me at that exact commentary as the precedent to follow.

## Spec excerpt

Add regression cases to `.agent-guild/scripts/test_check_job_spec.py` for the three changes T-001 makes to `check-job-spec.py`: R10's relaxed adjacency, the new R21 checker-routing rule, and R2's anchor-naming diagnostic.

**Each case must be drawn from the archived corpus** under `.agent-guild/state/archive/`, in the mutation style the suite already uses — take real shipped paperwork, change one line, assert the rule fires. The existing `M1`–`M6` cases are the pattern, and `copy_corpus_state`, `build_fixture_repo_root`, `mutate` and `add_weight_line` are the helpers they lean on. The archive carries real paperwork to mutate for all three, so a fixture invented for the occasion is not acceptable: a corpus somebody made up cannot demonstrate that real paperwork would have been caught. Note the R21 shape is reachable only by mutation — no archived task declares `checker: checker-deterministic` while citing a rubric clause, because until this job nothing refused one. Take a real archived task and change its `checker:` line; that is archive-drawn, and it is what the existing M1–M6 cases do.

**Each case must discriminate.** A case that stays green when its change is reverted proves nothing. Concretely:

- Reverting R10's relaxation — restoring the predicate to requiring the plural noun immediately after the number — must turn the suite red.
- Removing R21 must turn the suite red.
- Removing the anchor text from R2's diagnostic, while leaving every number it reports intact, must turn the suite red. At least one case therefore has to assert the anchor text itself appears, not merely that R2 fired.

Two things about exit codes, both consequences of #139: R2 and R10 are classified as *heuristics*, so a violation exits **4** rather than 1 and the message carries a `[heuristic]` tag. R21 is a proof and exits **1**. A case asserting the wrong code will fail.

The suite runs every script as a subprocess, so its cases exercise the real CLI contract — exit codes and stderr — rather than internals. Keep to that.

**Do not touch `.agent-guild/scripts/check-job-spec.py`**, which belongs to T-001, and do not regenerate any published tree.

**Do not commit anything.** C-7 checks this job's diff scope against the *uncommitted* working tree, and both queries it unions go blank once work is committed — a commit would leave that check passing on zero paths without inspecting anything.
